## Terms to secure (worth more than the headline discount)

- [ ] **Uplift cap** — target CPI-linked or 3-5% maximum
- [ ] **Seat-reduction right** at each anniversary without penalty
- [ ] **Price protection on expansion** — added seats at the same unit rate
- [ ] **Notice period** shortened to 30 days
- [ ] **Data export terms** — format, timeframe, cost, specified in contract
- [ ] Termination for convenience with pro-rata refund <if ACV > $100K>

## Pre-call checklist

- [ ] Notice deadline is diarised with a reminder 14 days before
- [ ] Internal exec briefed on target and walk-away before the first call
- [ ] Owning team has confirmed the target seat count is workable
- [ ] Vendor fiscal year-end identified
- [ ] Someone other than the tool's owner is in the room — owners negotiate poorly for tools they love
