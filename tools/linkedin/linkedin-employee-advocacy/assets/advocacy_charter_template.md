## 2. Taking part

- Taking part is voluntary. You join by telling [owner] yourself.
- You can pause or leave at any time by telling [owner]. You do not need to
  give a reason.
- Whether you take part, how often you post and how your posts perform are
  not part of performance reviews, pay, promotion or team targets.
- Your account, your connections and your posts are yours, and stay yours if
  you leave [company].
- Nobody at [company] will ask for your login or post from your account.

## 4. What is never scripted

Nobody will write these for you, and you should not accept them if offered:

- your opinions and your personal stories;
- praise for [company] or its products;
- reviews, ratings or testimonials;
- comments or reactions on colleagues' posts, or a schedule for making them;
- replies to criticism of [company];
- statements about competitors;
- text identical to what a colleague is posting.

## 6. Check before posting: triggers

Most posts need no check. If your draft contains any of the following, send it
to the named person first. They answer one question and do not edit your
style.

| If your draft includes | Send to | They answer |
|------------------------|---------|-------------|
| A customer, partner or supplier, named or identifiable | [ ] | Have they agreed to this mention? |
| A figure from internal systems | [ ] | Is it public and correct? |
| Anything not yet announced | [ ] | Is it public yet, or when? |
| A named competitor | [ ] | Is it factual and fair? |
| A legal, regulatory, safety or medical claim | [ ] | Is it accurate and permitted? |
| A colleague, named or pictured | That colleague | Do they agree? |
| An ongoing incident, dispute or investigation | [ ] | Should this wait? |
| [company-specific trigger] | [ ] | [ ] |

Answer time: within [one working day]. If you have not heard by then, send it
to [owner], who will answer the same day. No reply does not mean yes.

A checker may say yes, say no with a reason, ask for a fact to be corrected or
made less specific, or ask you to wait until a date. A checker may not rewrite
your opening, change your tone or add marketing language.

## 10. If you leave

Your account and audience go with you. You will be removed from the weekly
note on your last day. We will not ask you to delete posts unless one
disclosed something it should not have.

## 11. How we measure

- We report team totals, never a ranking of people.
- Sharing your own post figures is optional.
- We keep a log of outcomes (conversations, applications, introductions) with
  a note of how we know.
- We review the programme every [four] weeks with members and every quarter
  with [sponsor].
