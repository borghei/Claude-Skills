## Example of a filled seed (fictional)

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Person | Odalys Venter |
| Topic in their words | "Why I stopped asking for a route optimiser" |
| Pillar | ops-craft |
| Bank entries drawn on | S-004, S-002 |
| Naming for this post | clear |

1. **What happened.** In January 2022 she spent a week riding in truck 22
   from the Tilbrook depot. The truck was on the road on time every day and
   late out of the yard every day.
2. **What it cost or earned.** 37 spoilage claims in the first half of 2024,
   11 in the second half, after door-open time was capped at 6 minutes per
   load. Counts customer claims across three depots; known from the finance
   claims ledger.
3. **What I thought before.** "The route is where the minutes are lost."
4. **What I think now.** "The dock is where the minutes are lost; the route
   only shows it." Cause: seeing the same driver wait at the same door five
   mornings running.
5. **Who this is for.** Operations leads about to buy planning software.
   Disagreeing reader: anyone who sells or champions route optimisation, who
   would say both problems are real.
6. **What to try.** Time one dock door for one week before buying anything.

Their wording: "I had been optimising the part I could see on a map."
