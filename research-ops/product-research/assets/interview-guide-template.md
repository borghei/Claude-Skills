## 0. Opening — verbatim (2 min)

> Thanks for making time. I work on [product] and I'm trying to understand how
> [work area] actually happens day to day — not to demo anything or get feedback
> on a design. There are no wrong answers; I'm interested in what you actually
> do, including the parts that are messy.

> It won't be shared outside that. Is that OK?
>

## 1. Warm-up (3-5 min)

Goal: get them talking in specifics; capture the context for everything after.

- Walk me through what a normal [Tuesday] looks like for you.
- How long have you been doing this? What were you doing before?
- Who else is on your team, and how is the work divided?

---

## 2. Context (5-8 min)

Goal: establish the problem space in **their** vocabulary, before introducing any
of yours.

- Where does [the work] come from, and who hands it to you?
- What tools are involved from the moment it arrives to the moment it's done?
- What's changed about that in the last year?

## 3. Specific-instance core (20-30 min)

Goal: concrete past occurrences. This is where the value is. Never accept
"usually" — always convert to "the last time."

**Must ask:**
- Tell me about the last time [the situation under study] happened.

**Walk the timeline:**
- What triggered it? How did you notice?
- What did you do first?
- What did you try after that?
- When that didn't work, what then?
- Who else got involved?
- How did it end?
- How long did the whole thing take?
- What did it cost — your time, the customer, anything else?

**Then:** Was that a typical instance, or unusual? Tell me about one that went
differently.

**Probes available:** silence (3-5 seconds) · "Tell me more about that." ·
"What happened next?" · "Why does that matter to you?" ·
"You mentioned [X] — say more?" · "How did you learn to do it that way?" ·
"What would you have done if that hadn't worked?"

---

## 4. Workaround excavation (5-10 min)

Goal: find where someone cared enough to build an unsupported solution.

- Is there anything you do outside the system to make this work?
- Do you keep anything in a spreadsheet, notes app, or on paper?
- Has anyone on the team built something for this themselves?
- What do you check before you trust what the system tells you?
- Is there anything you've stopped using because it wasn't worth it?

---

## 5. Prioritisation (5 min)

Goal: force the trade-off. Unranked lists come back with everything important.

- Of everything we've discussed, which one — if it disappeared tomorrow — would
  change your week the most?
- If you could fix exactly one and the others stayed as they are, which?
- What would have to be true for you to change how you do this?

---

## 6. Close (2-3 min)

- What should I have asked you that I didn't?
- Who else should I be talking to about this?
- Would you be open to a follow-up if something comes up?

> Thank you — this was genuinely useful. [Incentive logistics.]
