# Disclosure Wording Library

Plain-language wording that satisfies the "clear and conspicuous" (FTC) and "clear and distinguishable" (EU AI Act Art. 50(5)) standards when placed correctly. Wording is only half the job — **placement** decides whether it works. None of these strings is legally mandated text; adapt to your market and have counsel approve the set once.

## Placement rules (apply to every string below)

| Rule | Why |
|------|-----|
| On the asset itself, visible at first exposure | EU Art. 50(5); FTC treats disclosures behind "more", in comments or in a link as avoidable |
| Same language as the ad | A German ad with an English "AI-generated" tag is not "readily understandable" |
| Same modality as the claim — on-screen for video, spoken for audio-only | FTC clear-and-conspicuous definition covers each medium used |
| Before the call to action, never after it | A disclosure after the click has done no work |
| Minimum contrast and duration: readable size, on screen long enough to read (rule of thumb: ≥3 s for ≤5 words) | "Technically present" is not "conspicuous" |
| Repeat per post, per placement | Viewers may not have seen the earlier post |

## AI-generated or manipulated media (EU AI Act Art. 50(4), NY, platforms)

| Situation | Short label (corner tag) | Long form (caption / end card) |
|-----------|--------------------------|--------------------------------|
| Fully AI-generated realistic video | `AI-generated` | "This video was created with AI. The scenes shown did not take place." |
| Real footage materially altered by AI | `AI-altered` | "Parts of this video were altered with AI." |
| AI voice (synthetic narrator, not a real person) | spoken: "AI voice" | "The narrator's voice is AI-generated." |
| Synthetic performer / AI avatar spokesperson | `AI performer` | "The person in this ad is AI-generated, not a real actor." |
| AI image of a real place or product in use | `AI image` | "Image generated with AI for illustration." |
| Evidently artistic or satirical work (EU exception) | end card: `Made with AI` | "Created with AI as a creative work." (placed so it does not spoil the piece) |

German: `KI-generiert` / French: `Généré par IA` / Spanish: `Generado con IA` / Italian: `Generato con IA` / Dutch: `Gegenereerd met AI`. Have a native speaker check the final set.

## AI-generated text on matters of public interest (EU Art. 50(4))

- Byline area: "Written with AI assistance and reviewed by [Name], [Role]." — use when you rely on the editorial-control exception (the label is then optional but builds trust).
- No human review: "This article was generated by AI." — at the top, not in the footer.

## Chatbots and voice agents (EU Art. 50(1))

- Chat opener: "Hi, I'm [Brand]'s AI assistant. I'm not a person, and I can make mistakes. Want a human? Type 'agent'."
- Outbound voice: first sentence must identify the AI: "Hello, this is an automated AI assistant calling on behalf of [Brand]."

## Endorsements and reviews (FTC 255 / 465, EU UCPD, UK DMCC)

| Connection | Wording at the start of the post or review |
|------------|--------------------------------------------|
| Paid post | `Ad` / `#ad` / "Paid partnership with [Brand]" |
| Free product | "[Brand] sent me this for free." |
| Discount or points for reviewing | "I got a discount from [Brand] for writing this review." |
| Employee | "I work at [Brand]." |
| Family relationship | "My [relation] owns [Brand]." |
| Affiliate link | "I earn a commission if you buy through this link." |
| Virtual influencer | "#ad — [Name] is a virtual character created by [Brand/Agency]." |

**Does not work:** "Thanks [Brand]", "#sp", "#collab" alone, "#partner" buried among ten hashtags, a disclosure only in the profile bio, a platform's built-in tag with nothing else (the FTC warns it might not be adequate).

## Review-page statement (EU UCPD Art. 7(6))

"We publish all reviews from verified buyers, positive and negative. Reviews are checked by [method: order match / purchase verification]. We do not pay for reviews. Reviews received in exchange for a free product are marked 'Incentivised'."

## Words to avoid in any disclosure

| Avoid | Why |
|-------|-----|
| "Enhanced", "optimised", "digitally improved" | Euphemisms — a reasonable viewer does not read them as "AI-generated" |
| "May contain AI" on everything | Blanket hedges train people to ignore the label and do not identify which asset is synthetic |
| Legalese ("synthetic media pursuant to...") | Fails "readily understandable" |
