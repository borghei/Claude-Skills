# delight — one considered moment

**Job:** add product character at a moment that earns it: a completed task, a recovered error, a
first success. Delight is specific to this product or it is noise.

**Run when:** the owner asks; a critique says the surface is correct but forgettable. Never routed
from the detector. The router drops it on operate and read surfaces unless asked.

## Change

- Write one sentence: what the person should feel, at which moment, and why that feeling belongs
  to this product. If the sentence could describe any product, start again.
- Pick the smallest carrier: a response to a meaningful action, product-specific wording, a
  transition with a recognisable material behaviour, a small illustration or sound grounded in the
  product's world, a discovery that reveals real utility.
- Scale it to frequency and stakes: a milestone may expand; a routine save just feels certain.

## Do not touch

- Speed and clarity of the task. Delight never delays, blocks or hides the primary action.
- Serious moments: money, deletion, privacy, access loss get warmth at most, never jokes.
- Defaults: no sound without consent, nothing mandatory or unskippable, nothing that tires on the
  hundredth repeat.
- Claims: delight copy invents no facts.

## Steps

1. Find the candidate moments (effort completed, wait, first use, recovery, mastery).
2. Choose one. Write the delight sentence.
3. Build it with the existing system; motion follows `animate` rules, copy follows `clarify` rules.

## Verify

- The moment could not ship unchanged on a neighbouring product.
- The task is as fast with the flourish as without it (time it).
- Muted, keyboard, touch, reduced-motion and translated paths all work.
- Repeat it ten times; it is still pleasant or it gets quieter.

## Hand-off

`polish`.
