Goal: classify one piece of customer feedback so the support lead can route it the same day.

Context: feedback comes from the in-app form of a project-management SaaS. The support lead
reads these labels each morning, so a wrong "urgent" costs them time and a missed one costs a
customer.

Task: assign exactly one category from: bug, billing, feature_request, praise, other.
Mark urgent as true only when the customer reports data loss, a payment failure or a full
outage, because those are the cases the lead escalates.

Output format: respond in JSON with keys "category" (one of the five values), "urgent"
(true or false) and "evidence" (the shortest quote from the feedback that justifies the label).

Example:
Input: "I was charged twice this month and can't reach anyone."
Output: {"category": "billing", "urgent": true, "evidence": "charged twice"}

Feedback:
{feedback}
