# Industry Adaptations

Industry-specific financial analysis considerations and key metrics.

## SaaS

- **Key metrics:** MRR, ARR, CAC, LTV, Churn Rate, Net Revenue Retention
- **Revenue recognition:** Subscription-based, deferred revenue tracking
- **Unit economics:** CAC payback period, LTV/CAC ratio (target: 3:1+)
- **Cohort analysis:** Retention and expansion revenue by signup cohort

## Retail

- **Key metrics:** Same-store sales, Revenue per square foot, Inventory turnover
- **Seasonal adjustment:** Apply seasonal factors in forecasting (Q4 holiday lift, Q1 reset)
- **Margin analysis:** Gross margin by product category and channel
- **Working capital:** Optimize inventory days + receivables days - payables days

## Manufacturing

- **Key metrics:** Gross margin by product line, Capacity utilization, COGS breakdown
- **Cost analysis:** Bill of materials cost analysis, absorption vs variable costing
- **CapEx planning:** Capital expenditure ROI and payback period
- **Efficiency:** Throughput, yield rates, scrap/rework costs

## Financial Services

- **Key metrics:** Net Interest Margin, Efficiency Ratio, ROA, Tier 1 Capital Ratio
- **Regulatory:** Capital adequacy requirements, stress testing
- **Provisioning:** Credit loss provisioning and reserve adequacy
- **Revenue mix:** Fee income analysis and diversification metrics

## Healthcare

- **Key metrics:** Revenue per patient, Payer mix, Days in A/R, Operating margin
- **Reimbursement:** Rate analysis by payer (Medicare, Medicaid, commercial)
- **Case mix:** Case Mix Index (CMI) impact on revenue
- **Compliance:** Compliance cost allocation and regulatory overhead
